description: "We separate the effects of galaxy structure and cluster environment on galaxy–galaxy strong lensing, finding that the discrepancy between observations and simulations reflects both insufficient central concentration in simulated members and differences in their locations within the cluster lensing field. A mass-conserving contraction that mimics core-collapsed self-interacting dark matter largely recovers the conditional lensing fractions, but does not resolve the difference in the fraction of members occupying cluster-critical regions."
